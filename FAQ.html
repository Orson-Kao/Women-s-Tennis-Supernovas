Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Im a beginner. Is that okay?

A: yes! we all start somewhere!

Q: do I need to bring a partner?

A: We make sure you are playing with people your level and provide appropriate partners.

Q: how would I find out what my level is?

A: schedule time to play with one of the organizers so we have a good idea of who a good doubles partner would be.

Q: how do I sign up?

A: Sign up on the spreadsheet. look for the tab "SN SUNDAYS"

Q: how do I pay?

A: venmo @zina-kao $5 for your first session $20/mo afterwards

Q: Are the SuperNovas part of a club?

A: While many of us are members of Chabot Canyon Racquet Club, SuperNovas are not affiliated with any club.

Q: any other things i should know about?

A: SuperNova Sunday is a very casual thing... we only ask that you not cancel same day and definitely don't no-show! join the whatsapp chat to find replacements if you can't play!
