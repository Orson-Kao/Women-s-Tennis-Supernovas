SuperNovas

Who we are

We are a women's group, dedicated to grow the Oakland 2.5-3.0 tennis community.
SuperNovas are open to all ages.

SuperNova Sundays

We play every Sunday
at the Chabot Public Courts from 10 - 11:30 AM.
Sign up on the spreadsheet, First come first serve
5$ per session, 20$ per month if you decide to play more.

Chat Group

Join the WhatsApp chat for lively tennis chat:

  • Arranging matches - singles or doubles
  • Discussing tennis spoilers
  • asking tennis advice: equipment, coaches, clinics
  • checking SuperNova Sunday court updates

USTA

We have women's 3.0 and 2.5 teams, as well as a mixed 5.5 teams.
All are welcome to join.

Other things we do

Because tennis is more fun with friends,
we are always looking for ways to facilitate and create good tennis vibes!
Potlucks, celebrations, and tennis get aways are some of the activities we do as a group.
